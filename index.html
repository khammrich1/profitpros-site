FAQ

Common questions

Do I need trading experience?

No. Start with the foundations and practice in a simulator. Experienced traders can jump straight into setups and trade reviews.

Why futures?

Futures like MNQ and MES trade nearly 24 hours on weekdays, have clear contract specs, and micros let you trade small size while you learn.

Will you tell me what to buy or sell?

No. Profit Pros is education and community. I share how I read the market and review reasoning, but I don't give signals or personal investment advice.

Do you work with prop firms?

How much money do I need?

None to learn. Use a simulator until your process is consistent, and only ever trade money you can afford to lose.
